The architecture document is at most three pages, built on the requirements it is given:

- the components, what each owns, and the boundaries between them;
- the data kept, where, and who owns it;
- the decisions taken, each with the alternative rejected and why;
- the risks and how the design contains them.

Stay inside what the intent and the documents you build on say. Where they are silent on a detail, write the assumption down as an assumption rather than inventing scope; where they leave open a decision, follow ASKING below.
