# Pause the game, and show the score when it ends

The game runs from the first key until it is won or lost, and a player who has to look away loses
their lives. Let the player pause and resume with the P key, and when the game is won or lost show
the final score on the canvas until a key restarts the game.

Pausing freezes pacman and the ghosts; a paused game eats nothing and loses nothing.
