# Once clearing the maze starts the next level, should the win outcome (CAP-1.5, CAP-3.1 YOU WIN screen) be removed, or kept for some other condition?

CAP-1.5 says the game is won once every dot and pellet is eaten, which contradicts CAP-5.1 to CAP-5.4. Either CAP-1.5 and CAP-3.1 go to Superseded with a migration, or they are reworded to a new win condition. Until this is answered both stay and conflict.

## Answer (agent:master)

Remove win entirely; the game only ends by losing all lives

The intent says that when the player eats every dot, the game should not simply end in a win. Instead the maze refills, the level goes up by one, and score and lives carry over. It names no other win condition and no final level. Removing the win outcome (CAP-1.5 and the CAP-3.1 YOU WIN screen) is therefore the direct reading, and it resolves the conflict with CAP-5.1 to CAP-5.4. Losing all lives is then the only way the game ends. This is also the smallest change, which suits a prototype.

Rejected:

- Keep win at a final level cap: The intent never mentions a final level or a cap. Adding one would invent a requirement and keep a win screen the intent moves away from.
- Keep win unchanged and make level progression optional: This contradicts the intent, which says clearing the maze always starts the next level, and it leaves CAP-1.5 in direct conflict with the level requirements.
